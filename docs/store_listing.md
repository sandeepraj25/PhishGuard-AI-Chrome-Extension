# Chrome Web Store listing — draft

**Name:** PhishGuard AI — Phishing Warning at Login

**Short description (≤132 chars):**
Warns you the moment you type a password or card number on a fake site, and tells you why in plain words. Checks run on your device.

**Category:** Productivity → Privacy & Security (choose the closest available)

**Detailed description:**

Phishing sites copy your bank's login page and disappear within hours — often before any blocklist knows about them.

PhishGuard stays quiet while you browse. When you click into a password, card or OTP field, it checks the site on your device and, if something is wrong, shows a clear warning with the reasons:

• "Contains 'yourbank' but is not yourbank.com"
• "Uses look-alike characters to imitate YourBank"
• "Domain registered 3 days ago"

What makes it different
• Warns at the right moment — no pop-ups while you just browse.
• Explains why, in plain language, in one glance.
• Private by design: pages are checked on your device. For known scam sites only an anonymous 4-byte fingerprint of the site name is sent — never the address you visit or your history.
• Covers global brands and Indian banks, UPI apps, couriers and government portals.
• "I trust this site" and "Report mistake" buttons put you in control.

Pro (optional): page-level and visual look-alike detection.

**Single purpose statement (for review):** Detect phishing pages and warn the user before they enter credentials or payment details.

**Permission justifications:** copy from `docs/permissions.md`.

**Data usage disclosures:** The extension does not sell or transfer user data; does not use data for purposes unrelated to its single purpose; does not collect browsing history. User-initiated reports contain only the site's registered domain and the risk score.
